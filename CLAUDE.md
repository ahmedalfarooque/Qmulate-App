## Binding rules (every session) — non-negotiable

These four rule sets bind every session and **override any conflicting or silent guidance** in `docs/`, the PRD, or the Obsidian vault. Where the vault currently contradicts them (see **Known vault/spec inconsistencies** below), the rule here wins until the vault is reconciled — never silently adopt a vault version over these.

### 1. Domain invariants (must always hold in every model, engine, schema, and migration)

- **Corpus and income are distinct and never mixed.** *Corpus* — **asl / أصل**: the endowed principal (the asset itself and any capital standing in its place) — is separate from *income* — **ghallah / غلة**: the yield the corpus produces. **Only income-class receipts may enter the distribution waterfall.** **Capital receipts are blocked from distribution** — this explicitly includes **sale proceeds and istibdal (substitution) proceeds**, which are corpus, not income, and flow back into corpus. A **non-diminution-of-corpus invariant always holds**: no operation may distribute, erode, or reclassify corpus as income. Every receipt is classified income-vs-capital at entry; the distribution engine consumes income only.
- **The Shart al-Waqif is immutable. Full stop.** The founder's conditions are write-once and **cannot be changed — not by a direct edit, a migration, a backfill, a "correction", and not by any approval, however complete.** *(Product-owner decision, 2026-07-27, restated unconditionally 2026-07-29. This rule previously read "amendable only via an explicit authority-gated reserved-matter workflow"; that wording is superseded — see [ADR-0006](docs/decisions/ADR-0006-shart-hatch-shut-in-e2.md).)* Authority-gated reserved-matter approval still governs the **other** reserved matters — istibdal and asset disposal, deed/certificate identity, access-matrix changes on a live endowment — but it is **not a key to the founder's conditions**, because there is no such key. If a competent authority or a court ever directs a change, the answer is a **superseding instrument recorded as a new record**, never an edit to the original: the table's purpose is to be the record of what the founder instructed.
- **On `SHART_INCOMPLETE`, the distribution engine refuses to compute.** If the conditions needed to resolve entitlement or shares are missing, ambiguous, or unrecognized, the engine **halts and returns `SHART_INCOMPLETE`**. It **never guesses, defaults, or infers the founder's intent** — resolution goes to the condition-interpretation path (living waqif, else the competent authority), never to code.

### 3. Staleness rule (regulatory figures are unverified until proven against primary law)

**Every numeric threshold, statutory deadline, fee percentage, and classification figure in this repo is UNVERIFIED until confirmed against primary Saudi law** (the Arabic regulation originals + Saudi counsel). This includes — not limited to — the SAR 200M / 50M classification bands, the 30 / 15 / 10-business-day deadlines, the 3-month post-fiscal-year-end distribution window, the ≥10-year retention period, and both the 10% (ʿushr) Nazir fee and the ≤10%-of-net-income Authority fee.

- **Flag on use** — when you state or apply any such figure, mark it *"verify — may be stale (confirm vs primary law)."*
- **Never present it as settled truth** in docs, UI copy, reports, or answers to the user.
- **Never hardcode it as final** — these live in configurable `Setting`s, so a correction is a config change, not a code change.

### 4. Decision boundary (you surface; you do not resolve)

**Every fiqh, legal, or scope decision is surfaced to the user with options and tradeoffs. You never resolve one yourself.** This covers any question of Islamic-law interpretation (entitlement/distribution logic, corpus/istibdal treatment, zakat), any point of Saudi law or regulatory obligation, and any product-scope call (phase in/out, MVP cuts). Present the options, the tradeoffs, and a recommendation — then **wait for the user's decision**; do not pick one and proceed. (Ordinary mechanical/technical choices with an obvious default proceed as usual — the boundary is specifically **fiqh + legal + scope**.)

## Domain glossary

Arabic terms that recur throughout `docs/domain/` and will show up in code/data models — keep them rather than translating away, they're the domain's actual vocabulary:

- **Waqf (وقف)** — an Islamic endowment: an asset permanently dedicated so its yield benefits specified beneficiaries or causes.
- **Waqif (واقف)** — the person who established the waqf.
- **Shart al-Waqif (شرط الواقف)** — the waqif's stipulated conditions: who benefits, in what order, and under what circumstances. Legally binding; a Nazir cannot deviate from it without authority approval. **Immutable — it cannot be changed at all, by any approval** (Binding rule 1). A change is recorded as a superseding instrument, never as an edit.
- **Nazir (ناظر)** — the trustee/administrator responsible for managing the waqf. This is the role QMULATE performs professionally.
- **Nazarah (النظارة)** — the body of work and legal duties the Nazir carries out.
- **Mustahiq / Mustahiqqun (مستحق / مستحقون)** — the beneficiary/beneficiaries entitled to the waqf's yield.
- **Ghallah / ʿAwa'id (غلة / عوائد)** — the **income**/yield/returns generated by the waqf's assets, distributed to beneficiaries. Distinct from corpus (**asl**); only ghallah is ever distributed (Binding rule 1).
- **Asl (أصل)** — the **corpus / preserved principal**: the endowed asset itself and any capital standing in its place. Never distributed; a non-diminution invariant holds. Sale and **istibdal** proceeds are corpus, not income.
- **Istibdal (استبدال)** — substitution of a corpus asset for a replacement; a reserved matter with ≤10-business-day Authority notice. Proceeds remain corpus.
- **Mawquf ʿalayh (الموقوف عليه)** — the beneficiary; the regulation's term for the *Mustahiq*.
- **ʿUshr (عُشر)** — literally "a tenth"; the customary 10%-of-revenue Nazir fee set by this engagement's deed (see NazirFee below). *(Figure unverified — Binding rule 3.)*
- **Ṣiyāna (صيانة)** — maintenance; reserved from yield **first**, before any operating cost, Nazir fee, or distribution.
- **Sakk al-Waqfiyya (صك الوقفية)** — the waqf deed, the founding legal document.
- **Al-Hay'a al-'Amma lil-Awqaf (الهيئة العامة للأوقاف)** — the Saudi General Authority for Awqaf, the regulator. Its digital platform is `digital.awqaf.gov.sa`.
- Waqfs are classified by the Authority as **large, medium, small, or direct-benefit** (كبيرة / متوسطة / صغيرة / ذات انتفاع مباشر) — several compliance obligations in `docs/domain/nazarah-regulation.md` only apply above a certain classification. ⚠ **THE PRODUCT RECORDS THESE AS TWO INDEPENDENT AXES, not one four-value field** (owner ruling 2026-08-25, landed S9-4a): `Waqf.classification` is **SIZE ONLY** (`LARGE | MEDIUM | SMALL | NOT_CLASSIFIED`) and direct use is `Waqf.directUtilization Boolean?`, whose **NULL means UNRECORDED and never "not direct use"** (a gate asked about a NULL attribute returns `DIRECT_USE_UNRECORDED` and the obligation is parked undecided, never guessed). The single enum made *a MEDIUM endowment in direct use* unrepresentable — recording it meant discarding one true fact. Migration 42 **REFUSES to apply** while any row still holds the retired value rather than remapping it (ADR-0004), and `DELETE` on a `waqf` row is separately refused, so the only way past that refusal is a per-endowment reclassification. **The four Authority categories above are the regulation's own wording and are unchanged — only this product's representation of them changed.**
- **Waqf types** (public-charitable خيري / private-family-*dhurri* أهلي·ذري / joint مشترك — **⚠ an Authority OVERSIGHT CATEGORY spanning both kinds, not a hybrid endowment; a waqf is never both, and the engine refuses `JOINT` — resolved, register item #11**; ⚠ **a ذري waqf that names a charity as its مآل / ultimate taker is NOT مشترك** — the charity takes only once the bloodline is over, R7) and **nature** (in-kind عيني / value قيمي), plus **descent along a line the deed continues** (ظهور / بطون) and the **order of entitlement**, drive eligibility and ghallah distribution — shares are not a flat list. **⚠ The primary key is the continuation stipulation, not the tier:** generational tiers (طبقات) place a person in the tree but **do not exclude** them, الأعلى فالأعلى is the **opt-in exception**, *tashrik* a third order, and the default is **lineage continuation, per capita over the living frontier of each line** ([ADR-0009](docs/decisions/ADR-0009-lineage-entitlement-and-no-joint-waqf.md)). The **canonical glossary** (Authority's official terms + these layer terms) is `docs/domain/glossary.md`.

## Suggested domain model (starting point, not gospel)

Derived from `docs/domain/unified-framework.md`'s three sections and the **real engagement structure**
observed in SharePoint (see `archive/raw-intake/linga-waqf-case/`, confidential). The hierarchy is
**Client (family) → Waqif(s) → Endowment/Waqf(s) → {records}** — one client can have several waqifs, and
one waqif several endowments.

- `Client` (family) — groups the engagement; has many `Waqif`s. (The first client holds **5** endowments — owner enumeration 2026-08-25, correcting the SharePoint-era "4 across 3 waqifs"; all five ذري, all five MEDIUM *provisional pending official valuation*, one individual nazir + one deputy across all five. Names/deed numbers live with the owner, never in this repo.)
- `Waqif` — an endower; has many `Waqf`s.
- `Waqf` (endowment) — id, waqif, deed/**certificate number(s)**, `classification` (large ≥ SAR 200M / medium 50–200M / small < 50M / direct-utilization), condition summary (Shart al-Waqif), fiscal-year. ⚠ **It must also carry the deed's مآل الوقف (reversion) clause** — the kind plus a link to the ultimate-taker beneficiaries — and the waqf-level `continuationStipulation`. **Neither exists in `schema.prisma` yet** (E3/E4); the engine leads by a declared delta, and neither may be given a default, because a defaulted reversion is a defaulted answer to *"where does this endowment go when the family ends?"*
- `TrusteeshipDeed` — the Nazir appointment, typed **primary** vs **authorized-representative** (delegated manager, jointly & severally liable — Nazarah reg. Art. 11(5)).
- `Asset` — property tied to a waqf: title-deed number, location, acquisition, valuation. A waqf can have multiple parcels.
- `Expropriation` — a government taking of endowed land: scope, compensation, and the **istibdal** (substitution) into a replacement asset + Authority notice (≤10 business days). *New — not in the original model; from the real folder taxonomy.*
- `Beneficiary` (Mustahiq) — tied to a waqf, share/entitlement, verification status, and **family-tree lineage** (branch/relationship). Carries the **UBO / beneficial-owner** dataset (identity, banking, share of proceeds) per the Beneficial Ownership Standards — sensitive PII. **⚠ NEEDS A REAL LINEAGE EDGE, and does not have one yet (ADR-0009 / register item #12).** Eligibility is now *by descent along a line the deed continues*, so the model must carry a **parent edge** (`parentId` self-relation — `null` = a child of the waqif, **never** "unknown") plus the **ẓuhūr/buṭūn link** (`SON` \| `DAUGHTER`; an *eligibility fact*, **not demographics**, read for one computation and never rendered as a person's gender), with **`tabaqa` derived from that edge and cross-checked** rather than trusted. `branch`/`relationshipAr` free text **cannot** express this. **A valid cohort is also constrained by the waqf's declared type** (register item #13): a `CHARITABLE_JIHA` beside a `FAMILY` member is refused (`COHORT_MIXES_CHARITABLE_AND_FAMILY`) **unless** the deed records that charity as the endowment's **ultimate taker**, and a `CHARITABLE_JIHA` on a `FAMILY_DHURRI` waqf is refused (`CHARITABLE_JIHA_ON_FAMILY_WAQF`) **unless the deed names it as the ultimate taker (مآل الوقف — R7, product owner 2026-08-10)**. On a `PUBLIC_CHARITABLE` waqf the mirror holds and is **absolute**: a member carrying a `lineageLink` is refused (`DESCENDANT_ON_CHARITABLE_WAQF`) and so is one carrying a `tabaqa` (`TABAQA_ON_CHARITABLE_WAQF`) — a خيري waqf has no bloodline and no generations. ⚠ **Those two mirrors are Claude's reading of R5, still awaiting owner confirmation.** ⚠ **And the model must be able to record مآل الوقف at the WAQF level** (a reversion kind plus a link to the ultimate-taker beneficiaries) — it currently cannot, which is E3/E4's debt; the taker itself carries **no `lineageLink` and no `tabaqa`**, because it is not a descendant. ⚠ **And per R-FRONTIER (product owner, 2026-08-03) each member's VITAL STATUS is an eligibility fact about the whole chain, not just about that person:** entitlement sits at the **nearest living point on each line**, so a member is entitled only if every ancestor strictly between them and the waqif is **deceased** — a living ancestor holds the entitlement and their descendants wait (`ENTITLEMENT_HELD_BY_LIVING_ANCESTOR`, **temporary**, reversing on that ancestor's death). The model must therefore make an ancestor's living/deceased state readable **along an ancestor walk**, not merely stored per row, and nothing downstream may cache or persist the exclusion as durable. ⚠ **And per R6 (product owner, 2026-08-03) the lineage edge is MANDATORY ON EVERY DEED, not only on lineage-order ones** — a `FAMILY` or `CATEGORY_ONLY` beneficiary with no `lineageLink` halts the run on `ORDERED` and `SHARED` too (`LINEAGE_LINK_MISSING`), because eligibility comes from descent whatever rule the deed uses and the engine will not pay someone it cannot place. **So `parentId` + `lineageLink` are not an optional lineage-mode extra: they are required columns**, and E3/E4 must also carry that into `data/fixtures/sample-waqf.json` — the seed can no longer ship an `ORDERED` (`waqf-001`) or `SHARED` (`waqf-002`) endowment whose members carry no edge. The full delta is written down in `packages/domain/src/distribution/__tests__/fixtures/README.md`. E3/E4 owes the migration; `@@index([waqfId, tabaqa])` also needs revisiting, since the hot query becomes an ancestor walk.
- `FinancialTransaction` — split into **Revenue** and **Expenses** (mirrors the folder taxonomy), on **dedicated** waqf bank accounts (no commingling), recorded in Arabic. **Every receipt is classified income-vs-capital at entry**; capital receipts (sale/istibdal proceeds) are corpus and are **blocked from `Distribution`** (Binding rule 1).
- `Distribution` — a **ghallah (income)** payout to beneficiaries; the waterfall is **ṣiyāna (maintenance) reserved first → operating → Nazir-fee → distributable**; within 3 months of FY end if no schedule set. Consumes income only — corpus is never distributed; refuses to compute on `SHART_INCOMPLETE` (Binding rule 1). **Entitlement is lineage-based and PER CAPITA by default** — equal per entitled head, recomputed each period, a deceased member's share **not** passing down their branch (ADR-0009). ⚠ **The cohort is the LIVING FRONTIER of each line, not every living descendant** (R-FRONTIER, product owner 2026-08-03): a member is entitled only if every ancestor strictly between them and the waqif is **deceased** — a living ancestor holds the entitlement and their descendants wait, temporarily, until that ancestor dies. Tier-exclusion applies only where the deed stipulates it, and a **joint waqf / mixed charitable+family cohort is refused**. ⚠ **A charitable jiha on a `FAMILY_DHURRI` waqf is refused UNLESS the deed records it as the endowment's ULTIMATE TAKER (مآل الوقف — R7, product owner 2026-08-10):** such a taker is **EXCLUDED by default**, receives **nothing while any descendant lives**, and takes the distributable **only once NO CONTINUING LINE is left** (R7-d, product owner 2026-08-11: *"over"* means no continuing line, **not** merely no survivor — so under `ZUHUR_ONLY` a grandchild through a deceased daughter no longer holds it) — at its **deed weight**, not a per-capita head share. Invariant **I-R1** asserts on every run that **no charity is ever paid a halala in the same run as any descendant**. ⚠ **An empty entitled cohort is NOT the same as a line ending:** a zero-weight living descendant, one held behind a living ancestor, and one withheld by a gate are all **NON-triggering** — the pool is retained and the run says so (`REVERSION_NOT_TRIGGERED_DESCENDANTS_LIVING`). ✓ **R7-D1 is CLOSED (owner-confirmed):** an **unenumerated `CATEGORY_ONLY` placeholder's** `active: false` **cannot certify a death** — it **holds** the reversion instead. ⚠ **Open and unasked:** the continuing-line trigger is scoped to `LINEAGE_CONTINUATION`; `ORDERED`/`SHARED` still use the strict reading (register item #13). - `ComplianceTask` — templated from the regulation's obligations, tagged by section (financial / operational / government_legal), **gated by classification**, with status tracking. The real activity-log is the first user-story source for this table's shape — do not copy its real rows, but its columns (workstream, category, task, status, dates, notes) are a good reference for the schema.
- `Document` — vault entry (deed, certificate, title deeds, trusteeship deeds, leases, valuations, financials, KYC/AML) with **≥10-year retention**, access matrix, audit trail.
- `GovernmentFilingStatus` — status tracking against Awqaf Digital, Baladi, Istihkam, Muqeem, Qiwa, Ejar. These are manual status fields, not live integrations — no public APIs are evidenced in the source material.
