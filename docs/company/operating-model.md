# QMULATE — Nazir Engagement Operating Model

> Structured summary of QMULATE's standard endowment-mandate operating model. Source: **Endowment Mandate
> Memorandum, V2.0, 2026** (`Qmulate 1.pptx`). *(Provenance note: the source file sits in a colleague's
> personal OneDrive / Teams chat, not the engagement folder; the client-specific instance is captured in
> the gitignored archive. This file keeps the **generic** model only.)*

QMULATE structures every endowment engagement around **two layers held together**:
- the **Legal Mandate** the Authority imposes on the Nazir (personally accountable), and
- the **Executive Mandate** QMULATE stands up to deliver it through controlled systems and **licensed
  subcontractors**.

QMULATE's own posture is **advisory, supervisory, and execution-coordination** unless expanded in writing.
Accountability remains **undivided with the Nazir**.

**Nazir remuneration:** set by the **waqf deed** (Nazarah reg. Art. 11), not a statutory rate. For the
current engagement the deed sets **10% of the waqf's revenue** (customary *ʿushr*), deducted from yield
before distribution. (The Awqaf Law's ≤10%-of-*net-income* figure is the *Authority's own* fee for
endowments not under its trusteeship — a separate thing.)

## The mandate in seven parts

1. **Discovery** — confirm the regulatory perimeter, the Authority **classification** (drives bylaws,
   audit, disclosure, training), the **Waqif's conditions** (supreme instrument; ambiguity → written
   interpretation first), and operational posture. *No property, distribution, investment, or subcontract
   activity begins until dedicated systems exist.*
2. **Legal Mandate** — the Nazir's statutory duties (registration, conditions, obligations, protection,
   distribution, investment, financial administration). See [`../domain/regulations/nazarah-regulation-en.md`](../domain/regulations/nazarah-regulation-en.md).
3. **Executive Mandate** — the controlled systems QMULATE stands up (below).
4. **Property Execution** — delivered via **licensed subcontractors** under QMULATE coordination (below).
5. **Authority & Limits** — **reserved matters** requiring prior approval (below).
6. **Performance & Reporting** — reporting cadence + zero-tolerance KPIs (below).
7. **Mandate Distribution & Ownership** — RACI: who owns what and the sequenced handover gates (below).

## Reserved matters (prior approval required)

Deviation from Waqif conditions · asset substitution · sale/pledge/gift/exchange/usufruct/easement/transfer
of property rights · long lease, litigation settlement, arrears compromise, or major capex · fundraising /
donation collection / unlicensed distribution channel · bank movement, commingling risk, or funds outside
dedicated accounts · non-Sharia-compliant or high-risk investment · disclosure of personal data outside the
approved access list · appointment of any system/vendor/subcontractor outside the mandate.

**Approval standard:** written principal approval + Saudi counsel review + competent-authority
approval/notice where required.

## Reporting cadence & zero-tolerance KPIs

- **Monthly → QMULATE:** operations, cash, rent roll, arrears, maintenance, risks, decisions, evidence pack.
- **Quarterly → Family Board:** financial statement, KPI dashboard, property status, beneficiary issues,
  compliance calendar. Plus a beneficiary **Q&A session**.
- **Annual → Beneficiaries & Authority:** audited financials or annual statement (by class); beneficiary
  disclosure; Authority filing. Plus a **General Meeting**.

**Zero-tolerance KPIs (no exceptions):** (1) on-time Authority registration & updates; (2) no commingling
incidents; (3) current beneficial-recipient KYC; (4) no missed AML/CTF suspicion reports; (5) no unlicensed
regulated real-estate activity.

## Accountability (RACI)

| Party | Role |
|---|---|
| **Family Board** | Principal — strategic direction & approval of reserved matters; guardian of the Waqif's intent. |
| **Nazir** | Accountable governor — full legal governance; **cannot delegate accountability**. |
| **QMULATE** | Mandate lead — systems, execution coordination, supervision, subcontractor selection, reporting. |
| **Saudi Counsel** | Legal review — interpretation, reserved-matter review, licensing perimeter. |
| **SOCPA Auditor** | Independent assurance — audit per the Authority classification. |
| **Licensed Subcontractors** | Day-to-day property execution within licensing perimeter. |
| **General Authority for Awqaf** | Regulator. |

## Sequenced handover gates (nothing downstream proceeds until the prior gate clears)

- **Gate 01 — Authority & Legal:** confirm classification; register the waqf & all assets; secure
  deed/certificate/title deeds in the vault; counsel review of reserved matters & licensing.
- **Gate 02 — Systems & Controls:** stand up E-01 identity/email, E-02 cloud accounting (SOCPA chart),
  dedicated bank accounts, E-03 ops workspace/compliance calendar, E-05/06 vault + dashboard.
- **Gate 03 — People, Property & Cadence:** build E-04 beneficiary registry + access matrix; appoint
  licensed subcontractors (Board approves); complete annual training; establish reporting cadence.

## Legal reference set (per the memorandum, checked 30 May 2026)

Nazarah regulation · General Authority for Awqaf Law · **REGA Real Estate Brokerage Law** + implementing
regulations · **Law of Real Estate Registration** · **Civil Transactions Law** · **Ejar** lease
documentation · **Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL)** · **SAMA Anti-Money-Laundering Law** · **Saudi
Authority for Accredited Valuers (Taqeem)**. *Arabic text of Saudi laws is controlling; counsel to confirm
amendments, licensing perimeter, and tax/zakat treatment before execution.*
