## Positioning

QMULATE is an **endowment-specific real-estate management company**. Its operating business is acting as
**professional Nazir (waqf trustee)** for family endowments under the General Authority for Awqaf's
Nazarah regulation. Its broader brand narrative — a multi-asset family-wealth / endowment / legacy
"family office" — is treated as **future-phase vision**, not current scope (see the BRD).

## Open item — registered office vs brand location

> [!warning] Riyadh vs Jeddah
> - **Legal / registered office: Jeddah** — this is authoritative (the CR certificate and the letterhead
>   both state Jeddah).
> - **Brand & the mandate memorandum say "Riyadh, KSA"** ("QMULATE Family Office · Riyadh").
> - The **General Authority for Awqaf** is itself headquartered in Riyadh (Awqaf Law, Art. 2), which may
>   explain the brand lean.
>
> **Resolution for now:** use **Jeddah** as the legal/registered office anywhere it has legal weight (e.g.
> contracts, filings, letterhead). Flag the discrepancy before publishing a marketing address. See the
> BRD "Open questions."
