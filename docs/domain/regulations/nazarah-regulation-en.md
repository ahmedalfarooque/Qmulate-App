# Regulation Organizing the Activities of Nazarah (Beholding) — English

> Structured summary. Source: `Regulation Organizing the Activities of Beholding.pdf`, SharePoint
> `References & Regulations/`. Issued under Council of Ministers Resolution No. (286) dated 21/05/1442 AH,
> pursuant to the Awqaf Law (M/11). The Arabic working transcription is in
> [`../nazarah-regulation.md`](../nazarah-regulation.md). Arabic is controlling. Summary only.

The core operating regulation for the **Nazir (beholder)**. It defines appointment, obligations, rights,
protection, disbursement, administration, finance, investment, oversight, and penalties. This English
copy resolves ambiguities in the Arabic transcription and is the reference for the BRD compliance matrix.

## Definitions & classification (Art. 1)

- **Beholding (Nazarah)** — the right to supervise/manage the waqf, protect its assets and proceeds,
  represent it, and implement the endower's conditions.
- **Substitution of the endowed asset (istibdal)** — replacing an endowed asset for a legitimate reason by
  transferring its value to another asset in the waqf's interest. *(Directly relevant to expropriation.)*
- **Classification by asset value:**

  | Class | Threshold (total endowed-asset value) |
  |---|---|
  | **Large** | **≥ SAR 200 million** |
  | **Medium** | **SAR 50M – < 200M** |
  | **Small** | **< SAR 50 million** |
  | **Direct-utilization** | beneficiary benefits directly from the asset itself |

## Appointment & register (Art. 4–7)

- Endower may act as Nazir or appoint one by name/description, and designate a successor in the waqf
  instrument. Appointment decision specifies **powers, remuneration, and responsibilities**.
- **Conditions (Art. 5):** Islam; legal capacity; suitable knowledge/qualifications; good conduct (no
  final conviction for dishonesty); not previously removed for lack of integrity; **Saudi nationality**
  where the endower is foreign and the asset is real property. A **legal person must be licensed** by the
  Authority. A **Register of Beholders** records qualified trustees (a registration committee to be formed
  within 2 years).
- **Termination (Art. 7):** resignation accepted; final removal decision/judgment; loss of an Art. 5
  condition; death; or other cases in the deed.

## Activities of Nazarah (Art. 8–20) — the obligations the platform must support

- **Art. 8 Registration & deadlines:** register the waqf + all assets within **30 business days** of
  documentation/effective date; update data within **15 business days** of certificate expiry or any
  material change (asset/beneficiaries/Nazir); operate under the confirmed classification.
- **Art. 9 Endower's conditions:** implement and never violate; interpret linguistically/Sharia/custom;
  reconcile conflicts (else favour closest to intent); **no deviation without competent-authority
  permission**; internal bylaws for large/medium.
- **Art. 10 Obligations:** comply with Sharia/law/SAMA transfer rules; make info available to
  beneficiaries; stakeholder communication channel; **collect & verify beneficial-owner data and keep it
  current**; periodic financial statements to beneficiaries (large/medium); **immediately report AML/CTF
  suspicion** to the General Department of Financial Intelligence (State Security) with confidentiality /
  no tipping-off; cross-border disbursement mechanism + Authority notice.
- **Art. 11 Rights of the Nazir:** **remuneration** commensurate with duties (set in the deed; if unset,
  request the competent authority to determine); legal standing to represent the waqf; access to
  documents; **may delegate certain functions** (delegate subject to Art. 5; **Nazir and delegate jointly
  and severally liable**) — this is the **Primary vs Authorized-representative** structure; supervise
  affiliated entities.
- **Art. 12 Protection:** preserve/represent the waqf; maintain & repair (may **deduct cost from proceeds
  before distribution**); **no asset substitution without permission** + notify Authority within **10
  business days** with details of the substitute asset.
- **Art. 13 Disbursement of proceeds:** per the deed's purposes and schedule, via **dedicated bank
  accounts**; if no schedule set, within **3 months** of the fiscal year-end; document all disbursements;
  a disbursing legal entity must be **licensed**; where no beneficiary is named/lapsed/general-charitable,
  follow endower intent and refer to the competent authority.
- **Art. 14 Administration:** internal policies (large/medium); internal financial controls; competent &
  trustworthy staff; **AML/CTF training**.
- **Art. 15 Financial management:** **dedicated bank account(s)** in the waqf's name, **no commingling**;
  record transactions **in Arabic**; orderly document-keeping; **audited statements (SOCPA)** for
  large/medium + budget estimate; **annual statement** for small (and direct-utilization where there is
  income/expense).
- **Art. 16 Investment:** Sharia-compliant; within acceptable risk; develop the endowed real estate to
  grow proceeds.
- **Art. 17 Dedication / Art. 18 Conflict of interest:** allocate sufficient time; **no self-dealing with
  relatives to the 2nd degree**; disclose conflicts.
- **Art. 19 Pledging:** prohibited without permission + Authority notice.
- **Art. 20 Record keeping:** retain all records (incl. local/international financial transactions) for
  **≥ 10 years**, with rapid access for the authorities.

## Oversight & penalties (Art. 21–27)

- **Art. 21–22:** provide the Authority requested information/reports; full cooperation with
  examinations; maintain confidentiality (survives the engagement).
- **Art. 24 Penalties:** warning; **fine ≤ SAR 500,000**; **suspension ≤ 90 days**; **dismissal** (e.g.
  crime via the waqf, violating disbursement conditions after notice, **commingling** without
  justification, fictitious transactions, refusing to open a bank account, refusing Authority
  examination).
- **Art. 25 Objections:** to the Administrative Court (warning/fine) or competent divisions
  (suspension/removal) within statutory windows.
- **Art. 27 Removal handover:** disclose all assets/records; hand over to the alternative Nazir; Authority
  notifies SAMA/CMA to revoke the removed Nazir's account authority. *(Grounds the "Previous Trusteeship
  Documentation" handover concept.)*

## Final provisions (Art. 28–32)

- Authority may exempt by type/size; may issue guidelines/templates; **1-year transitional** window to
  regularize; enters into force on Official Gazette publication.

## Product implications (traced in the BRD)

- Deadline engine: **30 / 15 / 10 business days**, **3-month** distribution, **10-year** retention.
- Classification (≥200M / 50–200M / <50M / direct) **drives** which obligations apply.
- **Primary + Authorized-representative** trustee model with joint liability → data model + roles.
- **istibdal / substitution** → the expropriation workflow (compensation → replacement asset → 10-day
  notice).
- Zero-tolerance controls: on-time registration, **no commingling**, current KYC/UBO, AML reporting,
  licensed real-estate activity.
