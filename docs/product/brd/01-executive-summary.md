# 01 · Executive Summary

## The business

**QMULATE** is a Jeddah-registered, endowment-specific real-estate management company (CR 7054453274) that acts
as **professional Nazir (waqf trustee)** for family endowments under the Kingdom's **General Authority for
Awqaf**. Its first secured client is a family with **four endowments** across three waqifs. QMULATE's
value is running endowments as an **institution** — documented, compliant, and reported — rather than the
informal, spreadsheet-and-goodwill way most family waqfs are administered.

## The problem

Being a Nazir is a **heavily regulated, deadline-driven, liability-bearing** role. The regulation imposes
hard obligations: register within 30 business days, update within 15, notify asset substitution within
10, distribute within 3 months of year-end, keep records 10 years, never commingle funds, verify
beneficial owners, report AML suspicion immediately, and file class-specific audited statements — with
penalties up to **dismissal** and **SAR 500,000** fines. Today this is coordinated across SharePoint
folders, documents, and manual tracking. That does not scale past one client and does not survive an
audit or a dispute.

## What we're building

A **platform that operationalizes the Nazir mandate end-to-end**, with two faces:

1. **Internal operations app** for QMULATE staff — endowments & deeds, beneficiaries & UBO/KYC, properties
   & leasing, **expropriations**, finances & distributions, a **compliance/deadline engine**, a document
   vault, and reporting dashboards.
2. **Client & beneficiary portal** for the Family Board and beneficiaries — self-service verification,
   visibility of statements and distributions, document upload, and on-record communication.

It is deliberately **endowment-focused**. The broader multi-asset "family office" vision is a documented
North Star, not this release.

## Why it will work

The requirements are not invented — they are **transcribed from the regulation and from QMULATE's own
mandate operating model** (the "systems QMULATE stands up": identity, finance, operations, beneficiary
CRM/KYC, document vault, reporting dashboard, training). The platform simply makes those systems real,
enforced, and auditable. See [05 · Operating model](05-operating-model.md).

## Success in one year (indicative — confirm in [03](03-vision-scope.md))

- 100% of statutory deadlines met on time (a zero-tolerance KPI).
- Zero commingling incidents; current KYC/UBO for every beneficiary.
- One client's core operations (compliance, beneficiaries, finance, distributions) live on the platform; onboarding a second is a matter of days, not months.
- Every distribution, filing, and decision is reconstructable from the audit trail.
