# 11 · Roadmap & Phasing

Indicative sequencing — **confirm with QMULATE** (see [10 · Q7](10-assumptions-open-questions.md)). Phasing
mirrors the mandate's **gated onboarding** (nothing downstream proceeds until the prior gate clears) so the
first real client can go live safely.

## Phase 1 — Compliance & operations core (MVP)

The minimum to operate one client compliantly and never miss a statutory duty.

- Endowment/deed records & classification, client→waqif→endowment hierarchy, trusteeship (primary +
  authorized-rep), **Nazir eligibility/residency** — BR-101–105, BR-109.
- **Asset register** (properties, parcels, deeds); **disposal/substitution as reserved matters** — BR-301, BR-306.
- **Internal bylaws for Large/Medium** — BR-606.
- Beneficiary & UBO/KYC registry with the minimum dataset, payment history & isolation — BR-201–206, BR-208, BR-210.
- Document vault with retention & access matrix — BR-701, BR-702.
- Dedicated accounts, revenue/expense in Arabic, reconciliation, **distribution engine**, cross-border
  disbursement, **internal financial controls** — BR-501–503, BR-505, BR-506, BR-511, BR-611.
- Compliance-task register, Authority registration/updates, filing-status tracking, **AML reporting**,
  **legal-matter tracking**, audit trail — BR-601–605, BR-607, BR-612.
- **Deadline engine** (30/15/10 bd, 3-month) with Hijri/Gregorian — BR-1001–1003.
- Compliance & financial dashboards — BR-901, BR-902.
- Gated onboarding, reserved-matter approvals, RACI, **first-client data migration** — BR-1101–1103, BR-1106.
- Non-functionals: NFR-01–07, NFR-09.

## Phase 2 — Client & beneficiary portal + reporting depth

- Beneficiary self-service (verify/refresh, statements, uploads, messaging) — BR-801, BR-802, BR-804,
  BR-806.
- Family Board view + reserved-matter approvals online — BR-803.
- Cadence reporting packs (monthly/quarterly/annual), beneficiary disclosure — BR-903, BR-805, BR-905.
- UBO disclosure workflow — BR-209.

## Phase 3 — Property operations, expropriation & subcontractors

- Property/lease/maintenance management & subcontractor coordination — BR-302, BR-303, BR-305.
- **Expropriation → compensation → istibdal** workflow — BR-401–404.
- Valuations; investment recording (pending products list); **asset development / capital projects** — BR-304, BR-307, BR-510.
- Nazir-fee automation & client invoicing — BR-507, BR-508; zakat/tax — BR-509.
- Handover/onboarding templates; training tracking — BR-609, BR-1104, BR-1105.
- Portfolio view & audit-ready evidence packs — BR-904, BR-906.

## Unscheduled / backlog

Requirements defined but not yet assigned to a phase — revisit each planning cycle and either schedule or
explicitly defer: **BR-106, BR-107, BR-108, BR-207, BR-608, BR-610, BR-613, BR-703, BR-704, BR-1004,
BR-1005; NFR-08, NFR-10–14.**

## North Star (out of scope here) — multi-asset family office

Beyond endowments: multi-asset wealth governance (equities, holding structures), standalone
brokerage/advisory, and serving the **businesses** and **individuals** segments. Captured as vision in
[`../../product/positioning.md`](../../product/positioning.md) and [02 · Business context](02-business-context.md);
**not** part of this build.
