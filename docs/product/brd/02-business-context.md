## The regulator & regime

The **General Authority for Awqaf** (HQ Riyadh; `awqaf.gov.sa`) supervises all public, private (family),
and joint endowments and oversees trustees. QMULATE operates under three instruments — summarized in
[`../../domain/regulations/`](../../domain/regulations/):

- **Awqaf Law** (Royal Decree M/11) — the Authority's mandate; the ≤10%-of-net-income fee benchmark;
  segregated-accounts and SOCPA-audit regime.
- **Nazarah regulation** — the Nazir's duties, deadlines, rights (incl. **remuneration** and
  **delegation**), penalties.
- **Beneficial Ownership Standards** (Dec 2025) — UBO identification, minimum data, verification,
  retention, disclosure; FATF Rec 25 alignment.

Adjacent laws the engagement touches (from the mandate memorandum): REGA real-estate brokerage law + Ejar
lease documentation, Real Estate Registration law, Civil Transactions law, **PDPL** (personal data), SAMA
AML law, and Taqeem (accredited valuers).

## How endowments are classified (drives obligations)

| Class | Threshold (total endowed-asset value) | Heaviest obligations |
|---|---|---|
| Large | ≥ SAR 200M | Internal bylaws, **SOCPA-audited** statements, budget, periodic beneficiary statements |
| Medium | SAR 50M – <200M | Same as large |
| Small | < SAR 50M | **Annual** financial statement |
| Direct-utilization | benefit direct from the asset | Annual statement **if** income/expense exists |

Classification is confirmed with the Authority during Discovery and **must be modeled** because it gates
which requirements apply.

## Commercial / engagement model

QMULATE earns a **Nazir remuneration**. The Nazarah regulation (Art. 11) sets it by the **waqf deed** (or
the Authority if the deed is silent) — it is **not** a fixed statutory percentage. **For this engagement,
the deed sets the Nazir's fee at 10% of the waqf's _revenue_** (the customary *ʿushr* / عُشر الغلة),
deducted from yield before distribution. (Separately, the Awqaf Law Art. 14 caps the **Authority's own**
management fee at ≤ 10% of *net income* for endowments **not** under its trusteeship — a different figure,
not QMULATE's.) The engagement runs as a paid mandate with a defined lifecycle, reporting cadence,
and reserved-matter approvals (see [05 · Operating model](05-operating-model.md)). Financially this means
the platform must handle:

- **Revenue** (rents, yields) and **expenses** (maintenance, operations, fees) per endowment.
- A **Nazir-fee / remuneration** line, calculable and deducted from proceeds **before** distribution
  (regulation permits deducting operating/maintenance/management cost from proceeds first).
- **Client invoicing** and engagement/SLA tracking for QMULATE's own commercials.

> [!note] Commercial specifics
> The **fee basis for this engagement is known** — 10% of the waqf's revenue, set by the deed. Fee **basis
> is configurable** in the platform (the next engagement's deed may differ). **Invoicing terms and SLA
> thresholds remain TBD** — see [10 · Open questions](10-assumptions-open-questions.md).
