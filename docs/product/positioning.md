# QMULATE — Positioning & voice

> Synthesized from `docs/brand/QMULATE-Brand-Guidelines-v1.0.pdf` and `docs/product/one-pager.md`. See `docs/brand/README.md` for where the brand documents disagree with each other.

## Positioning

"A family office, built to outlast its makers." QMULATE organizes multi-asset wealth — property, equities, and holding structures — and builds the governed systems that let value endure across generations, for businesses, families, and individuals in Saudi Arabia.

Three pillars, repeated consistently across every brand and product document:

- **Multi-asset oversight** — one coherent view across property, equities, and holdings.
- **Endowment structures** — vehicles designed to compound and protect over decades (this is where waqf/Nazarah work lives).
- **Legacy governance** — rules and reporting that carry intent between generations.

## Voice

Confident, specific, quiet — "structure does the persuading." Concretely:

- State positions plainly, without embellishment.
- Name the structure, the figure, the outcome — never "world-class" or "best-in-class."
- Short sentences, one idea at a time.
- Speak in generations and governance, not quarters and hype.
- No exclamation marks, no superlatives, no generic luxury language.

Sample lines the brand explicitly endorses: "Wealth, structured to outlast its makers." / "Multi-asset oversight, end to end." / "Governed systems that preserve value across generations."

## Segments (from the one-pager — more concrete than the brand deck's general "family office" framing)

Three segments × three service lines (full matrix in `docs/product/one-pager.md`): **Businesses**, **Families**, **Individuals**, crossed with **Wealth Governance & Reporting**, **Real Estate & Facility Management**, and **Brokerage, Advisory & Market Opportunities**.

## Open positioning questions

These aren't resolved by the source material — flag them to the business rather than assuming:

- **HQ city**: brand mockups consistently say Riyadh; the one-pager and the client's real case (Jeddah properties) say Jeddah. Possibly both are true (legal HQ vs. operating base).
- **Scope for v1 of the product**: the brand materials describe all three service lines as equally central, but the only line with real operating data behind it is waqf/Nazarah governance under "Wealth Governance & Reporting → Families." Worth deciding explicitly whether the SaaS starts narrow (Nazarah case management) or broad (all three lines, all three segments) — see `docs/architecture/` once that decision is made.
